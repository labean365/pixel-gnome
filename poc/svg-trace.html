PixelDrop — SVG Trace · Proof of Concept

Raster → SVG vectorization via ImageTracerJS. Drop a logo or flat illustration to see how it performs.

Tracing works best on flat, high-contrast art. Photos produce massive SVGs (thousands of paths) that are usually larger than the original — and email clients like Gmail and Outlook strip inline SVG anyway, so keep a PNG fallback for transactional use.
Good fitLogos · icons · monoline illustrations · screenshots of UI · simple banners
AvoidPhotographs · gradients · soft shadows · anti-aliased text at small sizes
